How to read this: LSE-D lower is better (lip/audio distance), LSE-C higher is better (sync confidence), CSIM higher is better (ArcFace identity), FID/FVD lower is better, SSIM/PSNR higher and LPIPS/LMD lower are better. NIQE lower is better (median over 3 fps face-centred crops). MOS is a human 1–5 score and is left manual. SSIM / PSNR / LPIPS / LMD only make sense in reconstruction (the output is compared with the original frames). Cross-audio runs are scored with no-reference metrics + identity + lip-sync.
Green = best value for that duration. — = not applicable / not computed. RTF = wall seconds ÷ audio seconds (lower is faster).
